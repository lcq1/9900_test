MEETING RESCUE BRIEF
Fictional office: 12 people, six unnecessary meetings per week.
Budget: 10 imaginary coins.
Goal: reduce meeting time without losing necessary decisions.
Constraints: accessible to quiet colleagues, no surveillance, easy to reverse.
Deliverable: a gadget name, a three-step workflow, one failure mode, and one measurable test.
Bonus: explain why adding glitter does not count as user research.
